I’m Einbaum Xu, a student at KIT, currently in my third semester. My long-term research focus is Agent Security — the security of systems that can plan, use tools and act on their surroundings.

This direction raises questions about trust and control: which instructions should an agent follow, what information can it trust, and where should its authority end? These are questions I want to explore as I develop my research interests.

My public projects span decision-making agents and interactive storytelling. Below are a Pokémon TCG agent portfolio and Where She Longs, a narrative adventure made with Godot. Each offers a different setting for thinking about decisions, interaction and their consequences.
